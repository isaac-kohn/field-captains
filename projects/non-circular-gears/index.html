Non-Circular Gears

By: Captain Isaac & Captain Campea

We are exploring how to generate non-circular gears for fdm printing.
This will be our first major video. We are submitting it for the Summer of Math Exposition contest.

So far, we have worked out how to computationally define the geometry and motion of arbitrary non-circular pitch curve pairs.

What is a pitch curve?
Without getting too technical, you can think of the pitch curve of a gear as its "base shape" before you add the teeth
